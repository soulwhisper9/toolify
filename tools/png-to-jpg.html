Frequently asked questions

Why convert PNG to JPG?
JPG files are much smaller than PNG for photographs. PNG is better for logos and graphics with transparency. Converting saves storage and loads faster on websites.
What happens to transparent backgrounds?
JPG does not support transparency. The background color you choose (default white) fills transparent areas. Change it to match your needs.
Can I convert multiple files at once?
Yes — select multiple PNG files and click Convert All. Each file is converted separately and available for individual download.
