Frequently asked questions

Which sizes do I really need?
Minimum: 16×16 (browser tab), 32×32 (bookmarks), 180×180 (Apple touch). For modern web apps: 192×192 and 512×512 (Android + PWA). Include all sizes for best support.
Do I need favicon.ico too?
A 32×32 PNG works in all modern browsers. Only very old Internet Explorer needs .ico format. For legacy support, convert the 32px PNG to ICO using a separate tool.
Best source image?
Square, 512×512 or larger, with clear focal point. Avoid tiny detail (won't be visible at 16×16). Solid background looks better than transparent for small sizes.
Is my image uploaded?
No. Everything runs in your browser.
