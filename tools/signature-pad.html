Frequently asked questions

Is this legally binding?
In most jurisdictions, an electronic signature has legal weight for contracts and agreements — but complex legal documents (real estate, wills, medical directives) may require notarization or specific e-signature platforms with audit trails.
Works on phone or tablet?
Yes. Touchscreen input is supported natively. Use a stylus for cleaner lines.
Why transparent PNG?
Lets you paste your signature over any background — PDF forms, contract images, or email signatures — without a white box around it.
Is my signature uploaded?
No. Everything stays on your device — the drawing never leaves your browser.
