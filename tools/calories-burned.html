Frequently asked questions

What is MET?
Metabolic Equivalent of Task — a way to measure exercise intensity. 1 MET = resting energy expenditure. 5 METs = 5× energy burned at rest. Higher MET = harder workout.
How accurate is this?
Formula: (MET × 3.5 × weight_kg) / 200 = calories per minute. This is a widely-accepted estimate. Real burn varies with fitness level, age, and effort — treat as a rough guide.
How much exercise per day?
WHO recommends 150-300 min/week moderate activity or 75-150 min vigorous. Aim for 30 minutes daily. Consistency matters more than intensity.
Does this include resting calories?
No, only activity calories. Your body always burns some calories (BMR). Use our BMR/TDEE tool for total daily burn.
