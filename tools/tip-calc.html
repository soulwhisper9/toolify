Frequently asked questions

What is the standard tip percentage?
USA: 18-22% (standard). UK/Europe: 10-15% often included as service charge. Middle East/Asia: 5-10% or often not expected. Check the bill first — service may already be included.
Should I tip on tax?
Convention is to tip on the pre-tax amount, but many people tip on the total for simplicity. Both are acceptable.
How to split unevenly?
This calculator splits evenly. For different orders per person, calculate each subtotal separately, apply the tip percentage, and add each person's share of tax.
Do delivery drivers get tips?
Yes, especially in the US. 10-20% of the order total is standard, minimum $2-5 for small orders.
