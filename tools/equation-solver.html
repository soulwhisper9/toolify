Frequently asked questions

What does the discriminant tell me?
The discriminant is b² − 4ac. Positive means two distinct real roots, zero means one repeated root, and negative means the roots are complex and the parabola never crosses the x-axis.
What is the quadratic formula?
x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a. It solves any quadratic, including ones that will not factor neatly. This tool applies it and shows each substitution.
Why do some systems have no solution?
If the two lines are parallel, they never meet and there is no solution. If they are the same line, every point on it works, so there are infinitely many. Both cases show up when a₁b₂ − a₂b₁ equals zero.
What is the vertex of a parabola?
The turning point, found at x = −b ÷ 2a. It is the minimum when a is positive and the maximum when a is negative, which makes it useful in optimisation problems.
