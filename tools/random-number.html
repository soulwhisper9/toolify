Frequently asked questions

How random is this?
Uses window.crypto.getRandomValues() — the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator. Suitable for raffles, contests, and any use where fairness matters.
Can I use this for a giveaway winner?
Yes. Number your entries 1 to N, then generate one random number in that range. That is your winner. Take a screenshot for proof.
What is "unique" mode?
Ensures no number appears twice. Useful when picking multiple winners or lottery numbers. Requires the range to be at least as large as the count.
Is my data uploaded?
No. All generation happens in your browser.
