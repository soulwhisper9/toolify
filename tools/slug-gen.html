Frequently asked questions

What is a URL slug?
A slug is the part of a URL that identifies a specific page in a readable way. For example, in "yoursite.com/blog/how-to-bake-cake" the slug is "how-to-bake-cake".
Why are short slugs better for SEO?
Shorter slugs (3-5 keywords) are easier to read, share, and remember. Google also gives slight preference to concise URLs that clearly describe the page content.
Should I remove stopwords?
Optional. Removing words like "a", "the", "of" makes slugs shorter and keyword-focused. But keep them if the meaning suffers ("history-of-rome" vs "history-rome").
Does it handle Unicode and non-English characters?
Yes. Accented characters (café → cafe) and Unicode text are normalized to ASCII where possible.
