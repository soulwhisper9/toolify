Frequently asked questions

What's a good WPM?
Average: 40 WPM. Good: 60+ WPM. Excellent: 80+ WPM. Fast typists: 100+ WPM. Professional data entry: 60-80 WPM required.
How is WPM calculated?
Standard: characters typed ÷ 5 (average word length) ÷ minutes elapsed. So 500 characters in 1 minute = 100 WPM.
How to type faster?
Practice touch typing (don't look at keyboard), use all 10 fingers, aim for accuracy first then speed. 15-30 minutes daily improves WPM steadily.
Is my data uploaded?
No. Everything runs in your browser.
