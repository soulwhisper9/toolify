Frequently asked questions

What is dividend yield?
Annual dividend as a percentage of share price. Yield = (annual dividend / price) × 100. A $150 stock paying $4.50/year = 3% yield.
What is a good dividend yield?
2-4%: solid dividend stocks. 4-6%: high-yield. Above 7%: check sustainability — very high yields sometimes signal falling stock price or unsustainable payouts.
Do dividends compound?
Only if reinvested (DRIP). Otherwise you get cash payouts. Reinvesting is one of the most powerful long-term wealth building tools.
Are dividends taxed?
Yes, in most countries. Rates vary — check your local tax laws. In the US, qualified dividends often get preferential rates.
