Frequently asked questions

What does this plagiarism checker detect?
It detects internal repetition, duplicate phrases within your own text, and calculates a uniqueness score based on vocabulary diversity and phrase patterns.
Can it check against websites?
This free tool checks internal patterns. For checking against the web, use Copyscape, Turnitin or Grammarly Premium which have access to web indexes.
What is a good originality score?
80%+ is considered good for most content. Academic writing typically requires 90%+. Below 60% suggests heavy repetition or very short unique vocabulary.
