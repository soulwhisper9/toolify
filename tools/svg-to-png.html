Frequently asked questions

Why convert SVG to PNG?
SVG is vector (scalable) but not supported everywhere. PNG is raster and works in all apps — social media uploads, older email clients, print, favicons, some CMS platforms.
What is scale multiplier?
Produces a higher-resolution PNG. 2× on retina/high-DPI screens keeps the image sharp. Use 3× or 4× for print or large displays.
Does it preserve transparency?
Yes. Choose Transparent background for a PNG with alpha channel — icon logos and cutouts work perfectly.
Is my SVG uploaded?
No. All conversion happens in your browser using canvas. Files never leave your device.
