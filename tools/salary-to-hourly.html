Frequently asked questions

How is annual salary converted to hourly?
Annual ÷ (weeks per year × hours per week). At 40 hours/week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours per year. So $60,000/year ÷ 2,080 = $28.85/hour.
Should I use 50 or 52 weeks per year?
Salaried employees typically use 52 (full year paid). Contractors often use 48-50 to account for unpaid vacation, sick days, and holidays.
Does this include taxes?
No, all figures are gross (pre-tax). Actual take-home depends on your country's tax rules. For Pakistan, use our FBR Tax Calculator to estimate after-tax income.
What if I work part-time?
Just change the "hours per week" to your actual number. All calculations adjust automatically.
