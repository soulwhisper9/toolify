Frequently asked questions

How to calculate percentage of a number?
Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. Example: 20% of 500 = (20×500)÷100 = 100.
How to calculate percentage change?
Subtract old from new, divide by old, multiply by 100. Example: 200 to 250 = (250-200)/200×100 = 25% increase.
