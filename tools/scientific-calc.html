Frequently asked questions

When should I use degrees versus radians?
Degrees suit geometry, trigonometry homework and navigation. Radians are standard in calculus, physics and programming. If a question mentions π in an angle, it almost always wants radians.
What is the difference between log and ln?
log is base 10, so log(1000) = 3. ln is the natural logarithm with base e ≈ 2.71828, used for growth, decay and compound processes.
Can I use my keyboard?
Yes. Numbers and operators type directly, Enter evaluates, Backspace deletes and Escape clears everything.
What do the M buttons do?
M+ adds the current result to memory, M− subtracts it, MR recalls the stored value into your expression and MC clears memory. A small indicator shows when memory holds a value.
