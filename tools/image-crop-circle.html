Frequently asked questions

Best profile picture size?
WhatsApp: 500×500. LinkedIn: 400×400 minimum. Twitter/X: 400×400. Instagram: 320×320. Use 800×800 or larger to look sharp on all platforms.
Why transparent background?
Transparent PNG means the corners around the circle are invisible. Perfect for platforms that display avatars with any background color.
Non-square images?
The image is auto-centered and cropped to a square first, then rounded. Best results with faces centered.
Is my image uploaded?
No. Everything runs in your browser.
