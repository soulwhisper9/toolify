Frequently asked questions

What is Unix timestamp?
Number of seconds since January 1, 1970 UTC (the "Unix epoch"). Used everywhere in programming, APIs, databases, and log files as a universal time format.
Seconds vs milliseconds?
Classic Unix timestamps are in seconds (10 digits like 1730000000). JavaScript and many modern APIs use milliseconds (13 digits like 1730000000000). Our auto-detect handles both.
Why UTC vs local time?
Timestamps are always in UTC (no time zone). We show both UTC and your browser's local time zone for reference.
Is my data uploaded?
No. All conversion runs in your browser.
