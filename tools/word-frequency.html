Frequently asked questions

What is keyword density?
The percentage a word appears relative to total words. Good SEO practice: 1-3% for focus keywords. Higher can look spammy to Google.
What are "stopwords"?
Common words like "the", "a", "is", "and" that add little meaning. SEO tools usually ignore these to focus on meaningful keywords.
Is my text uploaded?
No. All analysis runs in your browser.
Does it count multi-word phrases?
Currently only single words. For 2-3 word phrases (bigrams/trigrams), that feature is on our roadmap.
