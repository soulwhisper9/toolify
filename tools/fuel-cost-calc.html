Frequently asked questions

How is fuel cost calculated?
Fuel needed = Distance ÷ Mileage. Cost = Fuel needed × Price per liter. A 500 km trip at 15 km/L needs ~33.3 L. At Rs 280/L = Rs 9,333 total.
Where do I find my car's mileage?
Owner's manual, dashboard trip computer, or calculate: fill tank fully, note odometer, drive until near empty, fill again — kilometers driven ÷ liters filled = your real mileage.
Highway vs city mileage?
Highway is 20-40% better than city driving. Use highway mileage for long trips and city for daily commuting.
Does this include tolls or road fees?
No, only fuel. Add tolls, food, and lodging separately for total trip cost.
