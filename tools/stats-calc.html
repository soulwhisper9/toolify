Frequently asked questions

When should I use median instead of mean?
Use the median when your data has extreme values or is skewed, such as income or house prices. A single very large value drags the mean upward but barely moves the median, so the median better describes a typical case.
How are quartiles calculated here?
This uses linear interpolation between closest ranks, the same method as Excel's QUARTILE.INC and most statistics courses. Q1 sits at the 25th percentile and Q3 at the 75th.
How are outliers detected?
By the standard 1.5 × IQR rule. Any value below Q1 − 1.5×IQR or above Q3 + 1.5×IQR is flagged. This is the same rule that draws the whiskers on a box plot.
Is this sample or population statistics?
Variance and standard deviation use the sample formula with n−1 in the denominator, which is the usual choice when your data is a sample rather than the whole population. For population figures, use the dedicated standard deviation tool.
