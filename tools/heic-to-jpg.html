Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. All conversion happens inside your browser using heic2any. Photos are never sent anywhere.
What is HEIC and why do I need to convert it?
HEIC is Apple's default photo format on iPhone. Many websites, Windows apps, and older devices can't open HEIC files, so converting to JPG makes them universally compatible.
Will I lose photo quality?
JPG at 95% quality is visually identical to the original. Choose PNG for zero quality loss (larger file size).
Does it work on iPhone?
Yes, works on Safari on iPhone/iPad, Chrome on Android, and all desktop browsers.
