Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this AI detector?
This tool uses pattern analysis to estimate AI probability. No detector is 100% accurate. Use results as guidance, not absolute proof.
What AI models can it detect?
It detects writing patterns common to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and similar large language models.
Can AI-edited human text be detected?
Lightly edited human text usually scores lower on AI probability. Heavily AI-edited content may score higher depending on how much was changed.
Is my text stored?
No. Text is sent to the AI API for analysis only and is not stored on our servers.
