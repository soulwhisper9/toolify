Frequently asked questions

Which format should I use?
CODE128 works for almost any text. EAN-13 is for retail products (13 digits). UPC-A for USA retail (12 digits). CODE39 for older systems that need uppercase only.
Will my scanner read these barcodes?
Yes. Print at a size where the smallest bar is at least 0.33mm wide. Most phone barcode apps and dedicated scanners will read these easily.
Can I use these barcodes commercially?
Yes for internal use (inventory, tickets, tracking). For retail EAN/UPC barcodes to sell products in stores, you need registered numbers from GS1 — this tool just draws the visual barcode.
Is my data uploaded?
No. Barcode generation runs in your browser using the JsBarcode library.
