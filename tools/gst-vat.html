Frequently asked questions

What is GST?
Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a consumption tax added at the point of sale. Pakistan, India, Australia, Canada, and New Zealand use this term. Europe and the UK call the same thing VAT (Value Added Tax).
How is tax removed from a gross price?
Divide gross by (1 + rate/100). At 18% GST: gross of Rs 1180 ÷ 1.18 = Rs 1000 net. The Rs 180 difference is the tax portion.
What is the current GST rate in Pakistan?
Standard General Sales Tax in Pakistan is 18% (as of 2026). Some goods and services have reduced rates or exemptions. Always verify with FBR for exact rates on your goods.
Is this calculator accurate for tax filings?
Math is exact. But tax rules vary by product category and jurisdiction — for filings, consult a tax professional or your country's tax authority.
