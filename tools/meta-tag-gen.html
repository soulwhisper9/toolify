Frequently asked questions

Why do I need meta tags?
Meta tags tell Google what your page is about (SEO) and control how your links look when shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter. Without them, your shares often show broken previews or wrong info.
What is the ideal title and description length?
Title: 50-60 characters (Google truncates longer). Description: 150-160 characters. The character counter above turns red when you exceed these limits.
What size should the OG image be?
1200 x 630 pixels is the sweet spot for Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter summary_large_image. Under 8 MB in size. PNG or JPG.
Where do I paste the generated code?
Inside the <head></head> section of your HTML, before the closing </head> tag. On WordPress, use an SEO plugin like Yoast or All in One SEO.
