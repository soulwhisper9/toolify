Frequently asked questions

How is discount calculated?
Sale price = Original × (1 - discount/100). At 20% off, Rs 5000 becomes 5000 × 0.80 = Rs 4000. You save Rs 1000.
How do stacked discounts work?
Second discount applies to the already-discounted price, not the original. 20% + 10% is NOT 30% off — it's 28%. Retailers rarely stack, but coupons on top of sales usually do.
When is tax applied?
In most places, tax applies to the final discounted price. So after all discounts are applied, tax is added.
What is a good discount to wait for?
Electronics: 15-30% is common on sales, 40%+ is great. Clothing: 30-50% typical, 70%+ end-of-season. Cars: negotiate 5-10% off MSRP.
