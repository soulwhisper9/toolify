Frequently asked questions

How is overtime calculated?
In the US federal law, overtime kicks in after 40 hours/week at 1.5× regular pay. Some states use daily overtime (over 8 hours). Adjust the "overtime after" field for your rules.
Does it save my data?
No, everything stays in your browser. Refresh clears the table. Take a screenshot or download the result for records.
Overnight shifts (past midnight)?
Yes. If end time is earlier than start, it assumes the next day. E.g., 22:00 to 06:00 = 8 hours.
Is my data uploaded?
No. All calculations run in your browser.
