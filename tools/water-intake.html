Frequently asked questions

How is my water need calculated?
Base: 35ml per kg of body weight (research-backed for adults). Then adjusted up for exercise (extra 350-1000ml), hot climate (extra 350-1000ml), and pregnancy/breastfeeding.
Does coffee, tea, or juice count?
Yes, all fluids count toward hydration, including coffee (despite the diuretic myth), tea, milk, and juice. Water-rich foods like fruits and soups also contribute 20-30%.
Can I drink too much water?
Yes, but rare. Hyponatremia (low sodium from over-drinking) usually requires drinking many liters per hour. Follow thirst plus this target — do not force-drink beyond it.
Is this medical advice?
No, it is a general guide. People with kidney conditions, heart failure, or on certain medications should follow doctor's orders instead.
