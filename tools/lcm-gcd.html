Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between LCM and GCD?
The GCD, also called HCF, is the largest number that divides all your values evenly. The LCM is the smallest number that all of them divide into. For 12 and 18 the GCD is 6 and the LCM is 36.
When do I actually need these?
GCD simplifies fractions to lowest terms. LCM finds common denominators when adding fractions, and answers scheduling questions such as when two buses running every 12 and 18 minutes next depart together.
How does the Euclidean algorithm work?
Divide the larger number by the smaller and keep the remainder. Replace the pair with the smaller number and that remainder, then repeat. The last non-zero remainder is the GCD. It is dramatically faster than listing every factor.
What does coprime mean?
Two numbers are coprime when their GCD is 1, meaning they share no common factor other than 1. 8 and 15 are coprime even though neither is prime.
