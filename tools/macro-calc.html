Frequently asked questions

What macro split is best?
Depends on goal. Fat loss: high protein (30-40%). Muscle gain: balanced with adequate protein. Endurance: higher carbs. Keto: very low carbs, high fat. There's no universal "best" — pick what suits your life and adherence.
How much protein do I really need?
Sedentary: 0.8 g per kg body weight. Active: 1.2-1.6 g/kg. Building muscle: 1.6-2.2 g/kg. Losing weight while training: up to 2.4 g/kg to preserve muscle.
Do the percentages need to add to 100?
Yes, for accurate splits. The tool auto-normalizes if you're off. Custom mode lets you set values but they should sum to 100%.
Is this medical advice?
No. General fitness guidance only. Consult a registered dietitian for personalized nutrition plans, especially for medical conditions.
