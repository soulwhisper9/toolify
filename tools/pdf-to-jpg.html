Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?
No. All conversion happens inside your browser using pdf.js. Your file is never sent anywhere.
What resolution should I choose?
2x is good for most uses. Pick 3x for printing, 1x for previews and web use.
Can I convert password-protected PDFs?
Not currently. Remove the password from your PDF first.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, fully works on Android and iPhone browsers.
