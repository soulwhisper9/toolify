Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a permutation and a combination?
Order. A permutation treats ABC and CBA as two different results, so it suits race positions, passwords and seating plans. A combination treats them as the same, so it suits lottery draws, committee selection and pizza toppings.
When does repetition apply?
Repetition is allowed when the item goes back into the pool, such as digits in a PIN where 1111 is valid. It is not allowed when each item is used once, such as dealing cards from a deck.
Why is nCr always smaller than nPr?
Every combination corresponds to r! different orderings. Dividing nPr by r! collapses those into one, so nCr = nPr ÷ r!. The gap widens quickly as r grows.
Why do very large values show as infinity?
Factorials grow extremely fast. 170! is near the largest number JavaScript can represent, so anything beyond that overflows. For bigger cases you would need arbitrary-precision arithmetic.
