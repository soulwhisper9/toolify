Frequently asked questions

Why Impact font?
Impact became the "meme font" in the mid-2000s. Bold, condensed, and white text with black stroke is the classic look everyone recognizes.
Can I use any image?
Yes for personal use. For commercial or public sharing, respect copyright — use your own photos, free stock, or established meme templates that are fair-use in your region.
What resolution is best?
Output matches the uploaded image size. For social media, use 1080×1080 (Instagram), 1200×630 (Facebook/Twitter) as source images.
Is my image uploaded?
No. Everything runs in your browser.
