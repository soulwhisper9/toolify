Frequently asked questions

What image types work?
Any image format your browser reads: PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG. Best accuracy on non-lossy formats like PNG.
Hex vs RGB vs HSL?
Hex (#7B61FF): compact, web standard. RGB: values 0-255 for each channel, easy to modify. HSL: hue/saturation/lightness — best for color harmonies and shades.
Why sample from real images?
Matches an exact brand color, extracts palette from photography, recreates a design from a screenshot. More accurate than guessing from a screen.
Is my image uploaded?
No. Everything runs in your browser.
