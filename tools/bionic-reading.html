Frequently asked questions

What is bionic reading?
A typography method that emphasizes the first few letters of each word in bold. Your brain fills in the rest, letting you scan faster with less eye movement.
Does it actually make you read faster?
Research is mixed. Many people report faster comprehension especially for skimming. Others find it distracting. Try it on a few articles and see if it helps you.
Is it good for people with ADHD or dyslexia?
Many users report it helps focus and reduces eye tracking effort. Not a medical treatment, but worth trying if standard text is hard to sustain.
Can I paste this into Word or emails?
Use "Copy formatted" to keep the bold styling. "Copy HTML" gives the raw code for websites and blog posts.
