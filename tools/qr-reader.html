Frequently asked questions

What QR types are supported?
All standard QR codes: URLs, plain text, WiFi credentials, vCards (contacts), calendar events, phone numbers, email addresses, and payment codes.
Blurry or partial QR?
Works best on clear, high-contrast images. For blurry photos, try improving lighting or angle. Partial or damaged QRs may not decode.
Camera scanning?
This tool decodes images only. For live camera scanning, use your phone's built-in QR scanner or camera app.
Is my image uploaded?
No. Decoding runs in your browser using jsQR — nothing leaves your device.
