Frequently asked questions

What units does this use?
Whatever you enter. Put in metres and you get square metres for area and cubic metres for volume. The calculation is unit-agnostic, so just keep every dimension in the same unit.
What is the difference between volume and surface area?
Volume is the space inside, measured in cubic units, and tells you capacity such as how much water a tank holds. Surface area is the outside skin, measured in square units, and tells you how much paint or wrapping you need.
How do I find the area of an irregular shape?
Break it into shapes you recognise, calculate each one, then add them up. An L-shaped room is two rectangles. Subtract for holes, such as a circular pond inside a rectangular lawn.
Why does a cone hold exactly a third of a cylinder?
A cone with the same base radius and height as a cylinder has one third of its volume, which is why the formula carries the ⅓. The same relationship holds between a pyramid and a prism.
